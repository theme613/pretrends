# TrendRadar demo

## Pitch
Small business owners need new post ideas, but scrolling through popular content does not tell them how to use it for their own shop. TrendRadar helps them find a growing trend, understand its signal, and turn it into a simple business post.

## Research before implementation
- TikTok Creative Center already offers trend discovery across hashtags, songs, creators, and videos: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/popular/hashtag//
- TikTok's SMB Creative Playbook recommends exploring daily to spot emerging trends and understand how communities make them their own: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_SMB_Creative_Playbook_2025.pdf
- This supports the trend-discovery use case. It does not prove demand for this particular product or validate prediction accuracy.
- The user selected small business owners as the demo audience. A personal example has not been supplied; do not invent one.

## Focused demo
1. Explore a small collection of trends; filter by one to four topics, platform, or search. A video must match every selected topic. All ideas clears the topic selection.
2. Open a trend and compare earlier and later views.
3. Add a business name, edit a starter post hook, and copy the brief and shot list.
4. Bookmark useful ideas on the cards.

No login screen. The header contains only the logo, and the page starts with the trend collection. One focused experience with an editorial layout, instead of an analytics dashboard. The existing scrapers and API routes remain unchanged.

## Honest boundaries
- Existing t1–t30 seed records are sample data, including placeholder URLs. All are labelled sample; those URLs are not linked.
- Growth is calculated from the two stored view counts. There is no known collection interval, so no hourly growth, fabricated timeline, or peak date is shown.
- Signal score comes from existing data. It is not presented as a probability of success.
- Starter briefs use topic templates and the business name. They are explicitly labelled editable templates, not generated by an AI call.
- Bookmarks last for the current page session. They do not persist after a reload.
- Refresh reads GET /api/trends; it does not trigger scraping or claim a fresh live scan.

## Next validation
Ask three shop owners to use the demo with their own business. Can each find a relevant trend and make a useful post brief without help? Compare this flow with their normal scrolling process. Collect their own examples before expanding the product.
